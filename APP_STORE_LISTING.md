## Long Description

```
Purchase orders shouldn't require a $119/month subscription or a degree in demand forecasting.

StockyShift does one thing well: helps you generate purchase orders and stay on top of low stock. Period.

### How it works:
1. **Set reorder points** — tell us the minimum stock level for each product
2. **Get alerted** — when stock drops below threshold, it shows up on your dashboard
3. **Generate a PO** — select the products, pick a vendor, enter quantities
4. **Email your vendor** — PO is generated as PDF and sent directly to your supplier
5. **Receive against PO** — mark items as received (partial accepted), inventory updates automatically

### Built for merchants who:
- Run a physical goods business on Shopify
- Need to email purchase orders to suppliers
- Want low stock alerts without complex forecasting
- Are tired of paying $119+ for features they don't use
- Were affected by the Stocky shutdown (August 31, 2026)

### Simple pricing:
- One plan. One price. $29/month.
- 7-day free trial. No credit card required for trial.
- No usage limits. No per-order fees. No hidden costs.

### What makes StockyShift different:
- **Not a forecasting tool** — StockyShift is an operational PO tool. We don't pretend to predict your demand. You set the reorder points, we handle the execution.
- **Not an ERP** — we don't manage multi-channel sync, B2B portals, or accounting integration. We generate POs and email them. That's it.
- **Built for the 90%** — most merchants don't need AI demand planning. They need to know when stock is low and send a PO to their supplier. StockyShift is built for that workflow.

### FAQ

**Q: Is StockyShift a direct Stocky replacement?**
A: It covers the core Stocky workflow: reorder points, PO generation, vendor email, and receiving. It does not include forecasting, demand planning, or multi-location inventory management.

**Q: Do I need to migrate data from Stocky?**
A: No. You set your reorder points manually inside StockyShift. This takes 10-15 minutes for most stores.

**Q: Can I send POs to multiple vendors?**
A: Yes. You can have unlimited vendors. Current version generates one PO per vendor. Batch vendor ordering is in development.

**Q: Can I customize the PO template?**
A: Custom branding is not available yet. If you need custom PO templates, contact us — we're happy to discuss your needs.

## App Icon Design Brief

If you need an icon, here's what works:

- **Style:** Flat, simple, one main shape
- **Color:** Dark navy (#1a1a2e) background or white background with navy accent
- **Shape:** A document/paper icon with an arrow (representing PO sent) or a box/package icon
- **Avoid:** Clipboards, checkmarks, shopping carts (overused)

**DIY option:** Use Canva's app icon generator or [Hatchful](https://hatchful.shopify.com/) (Shopify's own logo tool) with the navy + white color scheme.

## App Store Review Prep

Shopify will review your app before it goes public. Common rejection reasons and fixes:

| Reason | Why | Fix |
|--------|-----|-----|
| **No privacy policy** | Required | Generate one, host at `/privacy` |
| **Screenshots don't match** | Showing features not in MVP | Only screenshot what actually works |
| **Broken OAuth** | Their test store can't install | Make sure dev store works end to end |
| **Test data only** | No real functionality | Ship working features first |
| **Pricing mismatch** | Listing says $29, trial says different | Keep consistent |

### Submission checklist:
- [ ] Privacy policy page hosted
- [ ] App icon uploaded
- [ ] 5 screenshots uploaded (matching actual features)
- [ ] Tested on a Shopify development store
- [ ] OAuth flow works
- [ ] Uninstall webhook returns 200
- [ ] No hardcoded test data
- [ ] "Contact us" email working

## The 5 Most Important Things for App Store Rankings

1. **First 10 reviews** — Ask every beta user for a review. Reply to each one within 24 hours.
2. **Keywords in title** — "StockyShift" + "Purchase Orders" in the app name helps.
3. **Update regularly** — Shopify ranks recently updated apps higher.
4. **Uninstall rate** — Keep it low (<5% monthly). This means: price fairly, deliver what you promise.
5. **Rating** — 4.5+ stars unlocks "Top Rated" badge. Every review counts at the start.

### Getting the first reviews:
When a merchant has been using your app for 2+ weeks, send a personal email:
```
Subject: Quick question about StockyShift

Hey [name],

Glad to see you're using StockyShift for [feature they use most].

If you're finding it useful, would you mind leaving a review on the App Store?
It makes a huge difference for a small app like mine.

[Direct review link]

If something's not working for you, just reply — I'd rather fix it than get a 3-star review.

Thanks,
[your name]
```
