## Detailed description (long)

### StockyShift — Stop running out of stock. Stop guessing what to reorder.

StockyShift is the simple reorder app for Shopify stores that don't want
spreadsheets. It watches your inventory against the reorder points you set,
tells you exactly what to buy and from whom, and turns it into a clean
purchase order in minutes.

#### Never miss a restock

- Set a reorder point for any product
- StockyShift shows you every product at or below its reorder point, with the
  quantity you need to order right now
- Get a friendly low-stock email every morning so you stay ahead — no
  dashboard babysitting required
- Sync is automatic (daily) and manual — one click and your products,
  variants, SKUs, and inventory levels are up to date

#### One-click purchase orders

- Create a purchase order from any low-stock product — the quantity to buy is
  pre-filled for you
- Multi-select products and create a single PO for everything you need to order
- Generate a clean PDF you can email, print, or share with your supplier as-is
- Export your products and purchase orders to CSV and work in your own tools

#### Keep suppliers straight

- Store your vendor information once per supplier
- See which supplier the product you're reordering belongs to
- Faster, more repetitive reordering — the same 5 products every week become a
  2-minute job

### Who it's for

Shopify stores that sell physical products — small and mid-size teams running
on spreadsheets today, and anyone who can't justify a $300/month inventory
system but is losing money to out-of-stock products.

### Data safety

- Your data is yours. Uninstall and it's gone (or restored if you reinstall
  within 48 hours).
- OAuth with a granular scope model: read/write inventory, read products and
  locations. No order, no customer, no payout data.
- Never share or sell your data. Standard GDPR data request and deletion.

### Pricing

- 7-day free trial, then **$29/month** — cancel anytime in two clicks from
  your Shopify Billing settings.
