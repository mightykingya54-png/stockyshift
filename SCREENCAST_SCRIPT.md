# StockyShift — Screencast Script (~4 min)

## Before recording (10 min prep)
In Shopify admin → Products, make sure you have:
| Product | Stock | Reorder point |
|---|---|---|
| Ceramic Mug | 2 | 5 |
| Linen T-Shirt | 4 | 8 |
| Canvas Tote | 15 | 10 |

Vendor: "Sunrise Ceramics" on the Mug/T-Shirt.
Mute notifications, close other tabs, hide the dock.
Record with Cmd+Shift+5 (portion of screen), 1080p.

---

## Scene 1 — Install (0:00–0:30)
**Screen:** Shopify admin → "Install app" page for StockyShift (unreviewed-app banner is fine).
**Action:** Hit record → click Install app → click Approve if prompted → wait for admin to reload.
**Narration:** "StockyShift lives right inside your Shopify admin — no setup, no configuration. Installed in under 30 seconds."
**Don't:** linger on the sidebar or show billing.

## Scene 2 — The problem (0:15–0:50)
**Screen:** Dashboard opens directly on the Low Stock view — Ceramic Mug shows "2" with a red warning badge, Linen T-Shirt "4". No clicks needed.
**Narration:** "And here's the first thing you see: everything you're about to run out of. Stock 2, reorder point 5 — flagged before a customer ever hits an out-of-stock page."
**Don't:** click around. Let the badges show 2–3 seconds.

## Scene 3 — Reorder points (0:50–1:30)
**Screen:** Products tab → click the reorder point on the T-Shirt → change to 8 → save.
**Narration:** "Each product has its own reorder point — your minimum healthy stock. Set it once; StockyShift watches from then on."

## Scene 4 — Vendors (1:30–1:45)
**Screen:** Vendors tab → Add Vendor → Sunrise Ceramics → contact@sunriseceramics.com → Save → show the row.
**Narration:** "Add your supplier once — name and email. Vendors are stored and reused on every purchase order."

## Scene 5 — One-click PO (1:45–2:40)
**Screen:** Low Stock → click "Order" on Ceramic Mug.
**Narration:** "One click turns low stock into a purchase order — quantities pre-filled from the deficit."
**Screen:** Show quantity 3 pre-filled, vendor Sunrise Ceramics → click "Create Purchase Order".
**Narration:** "No spreadsheets, no manual math. The quantity is already calculated."

## Scene 6 — The deliverable (2:40–3:25)
**Screen:** Purchase Orders tab → open the PO → click "Export CSV" → show the downloaded CSV file.
**Narration:** "Every PO exports as a clean CSV — ready to email to your supplier or keep for records."

## Scene 7 — Settings (3:25–3:45)
**Screen:** Settings tab — subscription card (Starter, $29.00/month, status).
**Narration:** "Pricing is simple: one plan, 7-day free trial, managed through Shopify."
**Check:** the monospace diagnostic line (DEV_SHOW_BILLING=...) must NOT be on screen.

## Scene 8 — Close (3:45–4:00)
**Screen:** Back to Low Stock tab or overview.
**Narration:** "StockyShift: never run out of stock again."
